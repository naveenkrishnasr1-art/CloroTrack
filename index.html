    How to test

    1. Dip the strip in water for the time on the pack, then shake off.
    2. Place it next to the white square of the reference card, in even light, within 30 seconds.
    3. Photograph, tap the white square, then tap the strip pad.
    4. Read the result and save it.

    Safety limits used

    At least 0.2 mg/L at the consumer's tap (BIS IS 10500:2012, WHO). Up to 1.0 mg/L is acceptable under BIS where no alternative source exists. WHO's health-based guideline value is 5 mg/L. Edit the LEVELS table in the code to match your state's rules.

    Limits of this tool

    Chlorine residual shows disinfection, not that water is free of chemicals or all germs. The built-in colour scale is a demo; calibrate it with your strip batch and standard solutions before relying on readings. Confirm any doubtful result in a lab.
